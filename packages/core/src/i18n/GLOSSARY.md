# Tamil glossary

The words every Tamil string uses the same way, so a customer or a garage
never reads one thing called two names. Owner's brief (10 Nov 2026): natural,
everyday Chennai Tamil that a car owner or a garage hand reads easily — not
formal literary Tamil, not machine-literal.

## How to write it

- **Keep the car-care words people already say in English**, written in Tamil
  script: சர்வீஸ், வாஷ், கேரேஜ், புக்கிங், ஸ்லாட், போட்டோ, ஆஃபர், கூப்பன்.
- **Actions in Tamil**, politely: the customer is asked with -ங்கள் / -வும்
  ("புக் செய்யுங்கள்", "சேமிக்கவும்"). The garage apps use the same politeness.
- **Never translate**: the brand "Detlio" (and Detlio Rewards, Detlio
  Guarantee, Shine Card, the Detlio Garage), garage / service / customer names
  and anything else the server sends, prices and figures (en-IN numerals,
  ₹1,500), "km", "min", UPI, OTP, GST, PAN, Aadhaar, WhatsApp, Google Maps.
  Month and day names stay English this phase ("Tue 20 Oct").
- **Keep every `{placeholder}`** exactly (the unit test fails otherwise), and
  move it to where Tamil puts it: `'Pay {amount}'` → `'{amount} செலுத்துங்கள்'`.
- **Short where English is short**: buttons, chips, tabs and badges have little
  room. Prefer the shorter natural word over a full sentence. A status chip
  sits beside a name and must leave it room (நடக்கிறது, not
  நடந்துகொண்டிருக்கிறது); the three-way mode switch has about 110px a label
  at 390px.
- **Option names stay in their own language**: "English" and "தமிழ் (Tamil)"
  are never translated, and "Language" is "மொழி (Language)" so someone who
  switched by mistake can find the way back.
